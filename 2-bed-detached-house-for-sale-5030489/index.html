4-Room Cottage on a 0.75-Acre Site

Located in pleasant rural countryside along a country laneway, this stone-built cottage needs significant upgrading but has considerable potential.

The cottage comprises four separate rooms. To the front is a large garden area that could have multiple uses. To the rear are two large blocks of sheds, divided into units of different sizes. The sheds offer potential for use as workshops or studios, or for conversion to residential accommodation, subject to any necessary permissions.

Gurteen village is within 4 km, while the larger towns of Tubbercurry and Ballymote are within a 15-minute drive. Knock International Airport is approximately 20 minutes away.
